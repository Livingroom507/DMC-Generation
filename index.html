Discussion & Education: Original Fashion Niche Business Plan

The "LIVINGROOM507's Business Plan" is a blueprint for launching an online fashion store using a dropshipping model. It strategically outlines high-demand products (dresses, activewear), reputable suppliers, and free marketing tactics (SEO, social media). The plan also provides a step-by-step guide for store setup, automation, and content creation, making it a valuable resource for aspiring entrepreneurs with a limited budget.
